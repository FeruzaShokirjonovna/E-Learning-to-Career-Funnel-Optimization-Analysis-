#  Studyflix User Engagement & Career Conversion Analytics
**An End-to-End Case Study on Optimizing the E-Learning to Jobs Funnel**
---

### 🏢 Project Overview
Studyflix is the largest free e-learning and career platform for students in the DACH region. It has a unique business model: students watch educational videos for free, and corporate clients pay to post job ads. To keep corporate clients happy, Studyflix must successfully guide students from watching videos to applying for jobs.

This project uses **Python (Pandas, NumPy)** and **Tableau** to analyze 1,000 student learning sessions. The goal is to find exactly where the platform loses users and how to increase job applications.

 ---

 ### Focus: 3 Business Metrics
To measure the success of the Studyflix platform, this project focuses on three core metrics:
1.  **Average Session Duration (ASD):** The average number of minutes a student spends watching a video per session.
2.  **Job Ad Click-Through Rate (CTR):** The percentage of students who click on a career advertisement after watching a video.
3.  **Application Volume per Category:** The total number of successful job applications generated by each educational subject.

---

### Key Trend Investigations & Business Insights
As data experts, we look beyond basic averages to find hidden user patterns. Here are the major trends discovered in this dataset:

#### 1. The 12-Minute Attention Breakpoint
*   **The Data:** The overall average watch time is around 10.5 minutes. 
*   **The Trend:** User behavior does not change at a steady, linear rate. Instead, there is a sharp behavioral pivot at the **12-minute mark**. 
*   **The Insight:** Students who leave before 12 minutes have a very low conversion rate of only **20%**. However, students who stay past 12 minutes become highly engaged, and their job ad click rate jumps to **70%**. 

#### 2. Subject-Specific Conversion Power
*   **The Data:** Economics, Mathematics, and Computer Science all have similar total view counts.
*   **The Trend:** Even though user volume is similar, **Economics content** creates a much higher intent to apply for jobs. 
*   **The Insight:** Economics alone generated **161 successful job clicks**, outperforming Computer Science (114 clicks) by over 40%. This shows that Economics students use the platform with a stronger career-seeking mindset.

### Data Pipeline & Architecture

#### Phase 1: Creating the Dataset (`dataset.py`)
Using Python, a script simulates realistic student workflows. It applies mathematical rules to link longer watch times with higher job ad click probabilities.

#### Phase 2: Data Cleaning (`clean_data.py`)
A verification script checks the dataset for missing fields or duplicate rows. The clean data ensures all calculations are 100% accurate before moving to visual tools.
